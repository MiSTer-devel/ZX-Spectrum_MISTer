Savestate Card

Savestate card manager for the MiSTer ZX Spectrum core.

Open the card (.sav) from the SD card, or drop it here. You can name the slots, take a game out as .z80, or put in a .z80, .sna or .szx snapshot by dropping it on a slot.

With POKEs… the card gets the cheats of a collection of .pok files (a .zip of them is fine), for the core's savestate window (P).

Nothing leaves your computer: the card is read and written by this page alone.
